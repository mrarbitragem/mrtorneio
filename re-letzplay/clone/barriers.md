### BR-001 Terms may restrict rebuilding the product
- Category: terms
- Blocks: F-001
- Gatekeeper: the product's operator
- Severity: adds lead time
- Market weight: required to sell
- What it takes: a reading of the terms before MRTorneio copies structure beyond what this study already uses as behavior notes
- Lead time and cost: [inf] Q-001
- Mitigation: rebuild the public draw from the rules in this KB. Do not copy logos, avatars, or page text.
- Evidence: [inf] Q-001

### BR-002 Player identity and ranking graph are not in the draw
- Category: moat
- Blocks: F-001
- Gatekeeper: the existing player network
- Severity: moat
- Market weight: required to sell a full circuit product. Not required to run one organizer's tournament.
- What it takes: profiles, historical matches and rankings were visible as other modules and were not studied
- Lead time and cost: [obs EV-001]
- Mitigation: MRTorneio keeps entries as names on a tournament. It does not need the social graph to show groups and a bracket.
- Evidence: [obs EV-001]

### BR-003 Charging and PIX sit beside the tournament and were not opened

- What it takes: not inspected
- Lead time and cost: [inf] Q-002
