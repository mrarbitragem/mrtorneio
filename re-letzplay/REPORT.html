R-001 A finished group lists pairs by place, not by a stats table

Given
a category whose group matches are all finished
When
the public Grupos/Chaves page is open on the group phase
Then
each group shows pairs ordered 1º, 2º, 3º with avatars and names, and the win-loss table is not on this page
Evidence: [obs EV-001]Feature: F-001 · Screen: S-001
ProbeInputResultEvidence

R-002 The tie-break link appears only when a group needed a later criterion

Given
several groups on the same page
When
some groups were separated by wins and others were tied on set balance
Then
Entenda o desempate is shown on the tied groups only
Evidence: [obs EV-001]Feature: F-003 · Screen: S-001
ProbeInputResultEvidence
Eight groupsGroups 02 and 06Those two groups carry the link. The others do not.[obs EV-001]

R-003 A tie of exactly two pairs is head-to-head; three or more use the balance cascade

Given
the criteria block on the group page
When
the published order is read
Then
a tie is the same win-loss balance. Two participants are split by confronto direto. Three or more use saldo de sets, saldo de games, aproveitamento of sets, aproveitamento of games, then sorteio. If that cascade later leaves only two tied, those two are also split by confronto direto.
Evidence: [obs EV-002]Feature: F-003 · Screen: S-001
ProbeInputResultEvidence
Criteria modal on a published group categoryRead, not submittedThe two-participant sentence names confronto direto. The three-or-more list is the five balance steps. A later sentence repeats head-to-head if the triple cascade leaves two.[obs EV-002]
Explanation of a three-way zero tieGroups that have not playedNarrative walks sets, games, set rate, game rate, then system draw. It also states that a two-player remainder uses head-to-head.[obs EV-002]

09Build plan

What to build and in what order, the acceptance tests, and what to copy, avoid and improve.

Updated: 2026-10-20 · Status: draft

Problem and users

Organizers and spectators of a beach-tennis category need the public page to show who finished where in each group, why a tie broke, and the knockout as a bracket. MRTorneio already computes standings and a knockout list. The missing product is that page and the publish step in front of it.

Launch blockers

From clone/barriers.md, severity blocks launch and adds lead time, with who clears each one and when.

BarrierSeverityOwnerStart by
BR-001adds lead timeProduct ownerBefore any public copy of layout or wording
BR-002moatProduct ownerAccept that profiles and rankings stay out of scope

Core loop

  1. Category is Grupo + Chaves with a group count and a number of qualifiers.
  2. Organizer generates matches, then publishes the bracket. Those are separate.
  3. Spectators open groups and see pairs in place order. A tie-break link appears only when a later criterion was used.
  4. Spectators open the bracket and see rounds in columns, with games and a tie-break digit.
  5. Horários lists the same matches by time, including group matches.

Scope by priority

P1: S-001 and S-002. P2: S-003 and the generate/publish split. P3: the other dispute models on S-006.

Acceptance

See clone/parity.md.

Out of scope for the first build

Finance, social actions, friend requests, king-of-the-court, Super 8 and Super 12.

Acceptance tests

Derived from the rules. Each test should fail on today's MRTorneio public category page and pass after the draw UI exists.

TestRuleExpectation
P-01R-001Public group view shows place, two names and no V-D table as the primary row
P-02R-002Tie-break control is present only on groups that used a criterion after wins
P-03R-003Two tied pairs are split by head-to-head. Three or more follow set balance, game balance, set rate, game rate, draw, and a later two-pair remainder returns to head-to-head
P-04R-004Opening the first knockout round still shows later rounds in columns
P-05R-005A 7-6 set renders games 6 with tie-break 5 against 7, not the digits 65
P-06R-006Horários lists group matches by time and court. The same matches stay on Horários after the group page moves them behind Visualizar
P-07R-007Generate and publish are two actions. An unpublished draw is not the public page
P-08R-008Four groups with two qualifiers seed 1st A vs 2nd C and the mirror fixtures
P-09R-012Before the group's results, its matches sit under the places. After every result, they sit behind Visualizar

Copy

  • Group page ordered by place, with the pair as the row.
  • Tie-break written in sentences, and only when the group needed it.
  • Bracket as columns of matches. Round codes R16, QF, SF.
  • Score with a small tie-break digit.
  • Separate generate and publish.
  • Cross-group seeding for 4 groups × 2 qualifiers.

Avoid

  • Logos, avatars from the production league, and the product's sentences as our own microcopy.
  • Friend requests, follow, and comments on a match.
  • A stats table as the thing spectators see first. The numbers belong in the explanation.
  • Head-to-head as a silent extra step until Q-003 is answered. The published list does not name it.

Improve

  • MRTorneio can keep the organizer score entry it already has. The public page should not be that form.
  • Say the criterion in Portuguese (saldo de games) instead of an internal code.
