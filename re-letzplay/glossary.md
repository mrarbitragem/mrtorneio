# Glossary

| Term | Definition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Categoria | One division inside an event. It has its own draw, entries and matches. | [obs EV-001] |
| Grupo + Chaves | Round robin in several groups, then a knockout of the qualifiers. | [obs EV-001] |
| Classificado | A pair that leaves the group into the bracket. Observed count: 2 per group. | [obs EV-001] |
| R16 | Round of 16. First knockout column on the category that was opened. | [obs EV-001] |
| QF | Quarterfinal. | [obs EV-001] |
| SF | Semifinal. | [obs EV-001] |
| Saldo de sets | Sets won minus sets lost. First tie-break step. | [obs EV-001] |
| Saldo de games | Games won minus games lost. Second step. | [obs EV-001] |
| Aproveitamento | Rate of sets or games won over the total played. Later steps. | [obs EV-001] |
| Publicar as chaves | Organizer action distinct from generating the matches. | [obs EV-001] |
